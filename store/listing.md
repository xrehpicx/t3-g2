## About (≤ 2000 characters)

Keep your coding agents moving from your glasses.

T3 G2 shows the threads you have pinned in T3 Code, live: which agents are working and for how long, what each one is doing right now, and their replies as they stream in. Hold the touchpad to send a voice message. It steers the running agent, and the agent transcribes your audio on your own machine.

- Pinned threads with live status: running with a timer, waiting for you, new reply, done, failed
- Read a thread with the newest messages at the bottom; scroll back and it stops following until you tap
- Answer an agent's questions and approve its commands, by tapping an option or saying your answer
- Start a new thread by voice, with the project, model and permissions you set on your phone
- Several machines at once, merged into one list
- Recording pauses instead of being lost when a notification needs a double tap

T3 G2 connects only to T3 Code servers you run yourself (T3 Code is free and open source). Pair it the same way as T3's mobile app: run `t3 auth pairing create` and enter the code. It has no servers, accounts or analytics of its own.

No server yet? Tap on the first screen to try the demo.

Open source: https://github.com/xrehpicx/t3-g2

## Privacy

**Policy:** https://github.com/xrehpicx/t3-g2/blob/main/PRIVACY.md

**Access permissions:** Microphone (voice messages, only while you hold the touchpad), Local network (your own T3 server)

**Data collection:** AI technology used. Your messages and voice recordings are sent to your own T3 Code server, where your AI coding agent processes them.

**Third-party services:** None. The app talks only to T3 Code servers you pair.

## Release notes (first version)

See your pinned T3 Code threads live on your glasses, answer your agents' questions, and talk to them by voice.

## Reviewer notes

The app needs a T3 Code server to connect to. To review without one, tap on the first screen ("Tap to try the demo"). The demo has sample threads, including one that is working live, one waiting for approval, and one asking two questions. Everything works with the glasses and ring alone: swipe to move, tap to open or choose, hold to record, double tap to go back. Double tap on the list exits.
